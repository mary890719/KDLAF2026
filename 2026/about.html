主題概念 Thematic Concept｜

《2026幻響》延續2025年關渡光藝術節的主題《幻響》，今年使用聲音與光的裝置與音像藝術演出，重新演繹光與聲響對於新媒體藝術的探索。透過一種幻覺的召喚，詮釋能量在不同感性作用對象之間所形成的抽象敘事，並從聲音、光線、空間與身體感知的交互關係中，展開對知覺邊界的重新想像。透過這種想像，讓現實與幻覺、具象與抽象之間產生持續流動的關係，並在每一次觀看與聆聽之中，形成屬於當下的感知景觀。


“2026 Illusonic” continues the theme of “Illusonic” from the 2025 Kuandu Light Art Festival. This year, the festival brings together sound and light installations alongside audiovisual performances, further exploring the possibilities of light and sound within new media art. Through an invocation of illusion, the works articulate abstract narratives formed by the ways energy acts upon different sensory subjects, while reimagining the boundaries of perception through the interplay of sound, light, space, and bodily experience. Within this imagined spectacle, reality and illusion, figuration and abstraction remain in a continuous state of flux, giving rise to a perceptual landscape that takes shape anew through each act of seeing and listening.
