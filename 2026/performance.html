音像表演及藝術家｜Audiovisual Performances & Artists

PROJECT FEED / ⌘ (insideworld)

蔡政廷｜Cheng-Ting Tsai

《PROJECT FEED》邀請了多名參與者各自錄下社群帳號瀏覽短影音的過程，並使用音料庫式串接合成（corpus-based concatenative synthesis）技術，嘗試透過混亂的重組，呈現出當代人不自覺被餵養的視聽景觀。
《⌘ (insideworld)》將冥想過後的繪畫進行動態轉換，透過多個重疊空間的運動，建構出滿載多彩幾何動態與奇異空間感的視覺場域，試圖運用數位工具的錯誤渲染作為通往抽象心理質地的路徑。

PROJECT FEED invited multiple participants in advance to each record themselves browsing short videos on their social media accounts, and used corpus-based concatenative synthesis to attempt, through chaotic reassembly, to present the audiovisual landscape that contemporary people are unconsciously fed.
⌘ (insideworld) transforms post-meditation drawings into motion, using the movement of multiple overlapping spaces to construct a visual field charged with colorful geometric dynamics and a strange sense of space. It attempts to use the rendering errors of digital tools as a pathway toward an abstract psychological texture.

《sonic_{imprints}°》

Jett Ilagan｜escuri

當我們在世界中移動、不斷進入陌生的環境時，我們如何適應這些新的所在？

《sonic_{imprints}°》以此提問為出發點，透過聲音與視覺展演，探索記憶、遷徙與都市生活如何在聲音經驗中交織。作品運用田野錄音、敘事與環境聲響質地，將城市經驗的節奏轉化為多聲道聲景、圖像記譜與生成式視覺。

作品源自藝術家的研究與個人經驗，思考聲音如何承載記憶、身分與離散經驗，並邀請觀眾聆聽都市生活中隱而未見的結構，以及城市如何在情感與聲音層面於我們身上留下印記。

How do we adjust to new environments we encounter as we move through the world?

sonic_{imprints}° investigates this inquiry through a sound and visual performance exploring memory, migration, and urban life through sonic experience. Using field recordings, narratives, and ambient textures, it translates the rhythms of city experience into multichannel soundscapes, graphical notations, and generative visuals.

Drawing from the artist’s research and personal experiences the project reflects on how sound carries memory, identity, and displacement, inviting audiences to attune to the hidden architectures of urban life and the ways cities imprint themselves emotionally and sonically.

Artist｜藝術家

Jett Ilagan
escuri

Jett Ilagan（又名 escuri）為菲律賓跨領域藝術家及文化工作者，現居臺北。其創作涵蓋裝置、錄像藝術與音像表演，關注環境聲音，尤其著重於「文化聲景」的概念。創作方法包含心理地理學、聲音漫步、社群工作坊，以及藝術家與特定環境及當地居民的親身交流，藉由沉浸式方法展開對聲音與場所的探索。
Ilagan 持續研究空間中的聲景，試圖促使人們重新思考自身與環境之間的關係。在人類活動深刻影響鄉村與都市生態及其生成空間的當代情境下，他透過聲音探索人、環境與空間彼此作用的方式。

Jett Ilagan, a.k.a. escuri, is a Filipino interdisciplinary artist, and a cultural worker based in Taipei. His body of works, which include installation, video art, and audio-visual performance, explore environmental sounds, particularly the idea of “cultural soundscapes,” through immersion-based methods such as psychogeography, sound walking, holding community workshops, and personal encounters with the subject environment and its locals.
Ilagan investigates spatial soundscapes, intending to encourage people to question and reflect on their relationship with the environment in this period, during which human activities have dominated rural & urban ecology and generative spaces.

《日常.zip｜Everyday.zip》

蕭力綺｜Li-Chi Hsiao

《日常.zip》將散落於生活中的影像、聲音與記憶片段，收集並壓縮進一座虛擬城市之中。
城市成為承載日常的容器：街道、建築與空間交疊著不同時間與地點的生活痕跡。那些原本零碎、彼此無關的片段，在虛擬空間中被重新排列，逐漸構成一座由日常所堆積而成的城市。
演出中，表演者透過感測手套捕捉雙手的動作，即時介入並操控虛擬城市中的影像與聲音。手勢在移動、觸碰與變形之間，將被收納其中的生活片段逐一釋放。
《日常.zip》是一個持續被寫入的生活壓縮檔，也是一場將它重新展開的即時音像表演。

Everyday.zip gathers fragments of images, sounds, and memories scattered throughout everyday life, collecting and compressing them into a virtual city.
The city becomes a container for the everyday, where streets, buildings, and spaces overlap with traces of life from different times and places. Fragments that were once scattered and unrelated are rearranged within this virtual space, gradually forming a city accumulated from everyday experience.
During the performance, the performer uses sensor-equipped gloves to capture the movements of both hands, manipulating the images and sounds of the virtual city in real time. Through gestures of movement, touch, and transformation, the fragments of everyday life stored within the city are gradually released.
Everyday.zip is both an archive of everyday life that is continuously being written and compressed, and a live audiovisual performance that unfolds it again in real time.

《聽｜Thiann》

吳承儒 ( P B )｜Cheng-Ru Wu (P B)

「Thiann」是台語中「聽」的意思。演出內容透過藝術家收集在臺灣的日常聲響與畫面，由攤販車的廣播或車輛的限速提示音等，透過數位的重混與調變，讓臺灣日常的印象透過聲景再現，並透過音像表演詮釋藝術家在臺灣生活的觀察與狀態。

“Thiann” derives from the Taiwanese word for “listen.” Through a collection of everyday sounds and visuals gathered across Taiwan—such as broadcasts from rural street vendors and vehicle speed alerts—the performance digitally remixes and modulates these elements to reconstruct daily Taiwanese impressions into a soundscape, interpreting the artist's personal observations and lived reality in Taiwan through live audiovisual performance.

《de-attractor》

何芯源｜Hsin-Yuan Ho

《de-attractor》以秩序解構為核心。空間初期由嚴謹的球體點雲構築而成，光點隨節奏律動緩緩交織。隨後聲響逐漸轉向細碎且不穩定的震盪，原先完整的幾何形態開始產生位移。從具象形狀分解成破碎遺骸，細碎的數位殘響在空間中層疊。粒子脫離原先軸心的束縛，向外逸散成不規則的紋路，記錄一段從生成、扭曲到散落的軌跡。

"de-attractor" is an audiovisual performance centered on the decomposition of order. Initially, the space is constructed from a rigorous spherical point cloud, with points of light slowly intertwining through rhythmic pulsations. Subsequently, the sound shifts toward unstable oscillations, and the once-coherent geometric forms begin to undergo displacement. Decomposing from concrete shapes into fractured remains, delicate digital echoes layer within the space. Particles break free from the constraints of their original axes, dispersing outward into irregular textures—documenting a trajectory from generation and distortion to dissipation.

《液態結構｜Liquid Structure》

王連晟｜Lien-cheng Wang

《液態結構》延續自前作《動態雕塑》對有限狀態自動機的探索，並進一步將關注轉向人工智慧生成聲音、數學算式所帶來的聲響變化。作品嘗試將聲音視為一種可被推演、流動與變形的結構，也成為影像、身體與空間之間持續變動的關係。
在影像部分以遊戲引擎建構即時生成，從後自然、虛擬星球到城市生成，探索有機、機械與虛擬空間之間的關係。作品透過聲音與影像的牽引與轉譯，使表演者、觀眾與視聽元素共同進入持續流動的感知結構。

Liquid Structure extends the exploration of finite-state automata developed in the earlier work Kinetic Sculpture, while shifting its focus toward AI-generated sound and the sonic transformations produced through mathematical formulas. The work approaches sound as a structure that can be computed, circulated, and transformed, while also becoming part of an evolving relationship between image, body, and space.
The visual component uses a game engine to generate imagery in real time, moving through post-natural environments, virtual planets, and processes of urban formation. These shifting scenes explore the relationships among organic forms, mechanical systems, and virtual space. Through the continuous interplay and translation between sound and image, the work brings performers, audiences, and audiovisual elements into a shared perceptual structure that remains in constant motion.

聲響藝術表演及藝術家｜Sound Art Performances & Performers

《01V96》

Stefan Tiefengraber

2020｜audio-video noise performance, variable size

《01V96》為 Stefan Tiefengraber 的音像藝術表演。藝術家運用「無輸入（no-input）」技法，將一台曾經廣泛使用、現已退役的數位混音台之內在噪訊構築出結構化的回授迴路。他將輸出端回接至輸入端，藉由內建數位處理觸發振盪與過載，使聲響生成節律、影像隨之閃爍。透過實驗性的路由與參數設定，作品擴展了混音台的既定功能，並引出未預期的結果；最終以寬頻聲譜與動態視覺投映於演出空間，營造沉浸式的視聽體驗。

01V96 is an audiovisual sound art performance by Stefan Tiefengraber. Using the no-input technique, the artist transforms the inherent noise of a digital audio mixer—once widely used but now outdated—into structured feedback loops. By reconnecting the mixer’s outputs to its inputs, internal digital processing causes signals to oscillate and overdrive, producing rhythmic sounds and flickering images. This expands the mixer’s intended function through experimental routing and settings, generating unexpected results. The resulting broad frequency spectrum and dynamic visuals are projected into the performance space, creating an immersive audiovisual experience.

《AN ENDLESS GOLDEN BRAID - A VARIATIONS》

PETER KUTIN feat. Florian Kindlinger

2025｜LED Light, Electromagnetic Sensors, Video, Sound, Stroboscope ; Site specific

這場特定場域（site-specific）的現場演出，延續了 Peter Kutin 於過去的作品《An Endless Golden Braid》中所發展的藝術策略。一道冷白色的 LED 光束產生不斷變化的電壓，並即時轉換為原始音訊，觸發多重聲音處理程序——將「光」直接轉化為「聲」。
此演出探索視覺與聽覺感知間的動態關係，讓「光」同時擔任樂器與指揮。在今年的關渡光藝術節中，Peter Kutin 與 Florian Kindlinger 將聯手呈現這件特定場域創作，交織科技、場域與感官經驗，帶來一場極具臨場感的聲光饗宴。

This site-specific live performance builds on the artistic strategies explored in Peter Kutin’s earlier work An Endless Golden Braid. A line of cold-white LED light generates shifting voltages, which are translated in real time into raw audio that functions as trigger for diverse audio processes — transforming light directly into sound. The performance explores the dynamic relationship between visual and sonic perception, where illumination becomes both instrument and conductor. For this year’s Kuan Du Light Art Festival, Peter Kutin and Florian Kindlinger will present a site specific work, merging technology, environment, and sensory experience into a an audiovisual composition.

《Magnetic Space》

Félix-Antoine Morin

2025｜Live music

Félix-Antoine Morin 是來自加拿大的聲音藝術家與作曲家，他的創作源於電子聲響作曲，並融合傳統與神聖音樂的儀式邏輯，探索聲音、影像與空間之間的流動界線。在他的作品中，聲音如咒語般逐層展演與變形，引導觀眾進入一場感知與深度聆聽的沉浸旅程。他運用聲音心理學與尺度變化，形塑出充滿詩意的多感官感知場域，重新想像現實。
此次展演 Morin 與加拿大實驗電影導演 Alexandre Larose 合作，Larose 的影像作品擅長以貼近類比膠片的工藝手法，無使用 CG 效果，透過自製裝置與特殊色彩處理，創造出如夢似幻、卻源自現實的影像質地。兩人的協作將聲響與光影交織，召喚出一場詩意與感官交錯的現場構成，邀請觀眾置身於現實與夢境交融的磁性空間中。

Félix-Antoine Morin is a Canadian sound artist and composer whose work transforms reality into immersive electroacoustic experiences. Drawing from ritualistic structures inspired by traditional and sacred music, his compositions evolve through layered processes of transformation, engaging psychoacoustics, scale, and sonic subjectivity. His multisensory environments invite deep listening and poetic reimagination.
For Magnetic Space, Morin collaborates with Canadian experimental filmmaker Alexandre Larose, known for crafting dreamlike imagery using analog-based techniques. Eschewing CGI, Larose builds custom devices and develops film processing methods to generate impossible yet real images—melding memory, dream, and perception through tactile visuality. Their collaboration weaves sound and vision into a hypnotic live composition, enveloping the audience in a spatial and sensory experience that blurs the line between reality and illusion.

《Epsilon: 溯｜Epsilon: Recur》

陳智彦｜Chan Chi Yin

2025｜3D列印聚合物、木、漆包線、麻繩、環氧樹脂、感測器、燈條、電子零件

This performance is constructed from a body-driven engine of sound and light.
At its core: three hand-made electromagnetic sensors, with a wearable interface translating touch and form.
Under the Sisyphean gaze, body becomes medium.
Body, device, sound, and light—hurled into the circuit.
Fingers etch; posture paints.
Capture the present. Operate on memory.
Replay, reforge, erase.
Through endless repetition, an imprint is done.

《殘稿｜Remaining Manuscript》

劉東昱 X 莊勝凱｜WhereIsChappie x ShengKai

2025｜音像

數位與自然之間不可見的筆跡，在時間中反覆書寫、抹去與再生成，如同記憶在轉譯的過程裡變形、模糊。人為與自然的紀錄彼此滲透，成為連續卻不穩定的脈動，類比與數位的資料交錯流動，使遺留的記錄與現實無法完全對齊。每一次觀測殘稿，皆使痕跡產生偏移，於偏移之中形塑新的記憶。

Invisible traces between the digital and the natural are written, erased, and rewritten over time—like memories transforming and fading through translation. Human and natural records permeate each other, forming continuous yet unstable pulses. Analog and digital data flow and intertwine, leaving recorded remnants never fully aligned with reality. Each observation of the remaining manuscript shifts its traces, shaping new memories within the act of deviation.

藝術家的即興聲音表演以一台經過解構的手風琴為核心，透過電聲處理展開聲響探索。從自由簧片震動所產生的聲波為起點，藉由多重物理延伸與聲學、電磁傳感器的介入，生成彎曲的群集持音、共振的低頻與強烈的金屬聲響。這件自我改造的樂器具有不穩定性，使表演成為一場聲音與身體之間的協商過程：藝術家在不完全可控的聲響之中傾聽、回應，於即興之間形構動態的聽覺場域。

The artist’s improvised sound performances are grounded in the electroacoustic treatment of a deconstructed piano accordion. Beginning with acoustic drones generated by vibrating free reeds, a network of physical extensions and various acoustic and electromagnetic transducers creates bending cluster tones, sympathetic resonances, sub-bass waves, and metallic excursions. The inherent instability of this self-modified instrument establishes a state of negotiation and heightened responsiveness, as the artist follows and reacts to sounds that are never entirely under control—shaping a dynamic and evolving field of listening.

《視感認知｜Mind Fluid》

吳承儒｜Wu Cheng-Ru

2025｜音像表演

創作核心為探討數位影像的觀看的方式，在數位影像的構成中，都是由許多像素(Pixel)所構成的內容，並透過我們的認知去投射對象物，也因此在數位世界中的觀看，將影像的延伸與變形背後，其實剩下的都是像素(Pixel)中的色塊，並呼應在這個生成式影像發達的世代裡，在觀看許多生成式的影像背後，終究是由噪訊(Noise)所重組而成的，也因此透過在音像的演出過程中，藉由過程來詮釋數位影像的建構與拆解過程，並以此發想作為衍伸。

The core idea of this project is to explore the way we look at digital images.
Every digital image is made of many pixels, and through our perception we project meaning onto the objects we see. In the digital world, when images are extended or transformed, what remains are simply blocks of color inside the pixels.
This concept also reflects our current age of generative images, where many visuals we see are ultimately reconstructed from noise. Therefore, in our audiovisual performance, we interpret this process by showing the construction and deconstruction of digital images, and develop our creation from this idea.
